How I Work

Clear validation before big claims

  • Write clear repro steps, expected results, actual results, severity, priority, and player impact.
  • Use smoke, regression, and fix verification habits to keep changes grounded in observable behavior.
  • Turn repeated QA friction into small tools, examples, tests, and documentation.
  • Use AI assistance for planning, drafting, review, and reflection while keeping human judgment visible.
